Accuracy

Measured values are the elevations you entered. Projected values are interpolated from the calibration and are estimates only. Elevations apply along the line of sight shown — an elevation line says nothing about the ground to its left or right. This is not a survey instrument. Recalibrate against a known point whenever you can.

Sends the camera height, wall height, distance and grade to the photo ruler. It assumes the photo was taken from the observation point.

The curb assumption

The curb is taken to be level with where you stand, which is what turns its shot into your instrument height. Where that is not true — a street that falls past the house — switch to a measured eye height instead.
